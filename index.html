Top Science-Backed Longevity Supplements

1. Nicotinamide Mononucleotide (NMN)

NMN is a precursor to NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide), a molecule essential for cellular energy production and repair. As we age, NAD+ levels naturally decline, leading to reduced energy and increased signs of aging.

  • Enhances focus and mental clarity

  • Supports DNA repair mechanisms

Resveratrol is a powerful antioxidant found in red wine and certain plants. It has been linked to improved cardiovascular health and anti-aging benefits by activating sirtuins-proteins that regulate cellular health.

Benefits of Resveratrol:

  • Promotes cardiovascular health

  • Supports healthy aging processes
